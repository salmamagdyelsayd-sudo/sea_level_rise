Why is this a big problem?

Sea level rise is not the same everywhere. In the Nile Delta, two things happen at the same time: the sea goes up and the land goes down. Together, they make the risk much bigger.

Why is the land sinking?

After the Aswan High Dam was built, river sand and mud stopped reaching the coast. The coast cannot rebuild itself, so it erodes. At the same time, the ground in the Delta is getting lower. Subsidence is 7.7 mm/year at Burullus and 8.4 mm/year at Port Said.

El-Geziry (2020); Zahra (2026)

What can we do?

There is no single answer. The best plan mixes three ideas inside an Integrated Coastal Zone Management (ICZM) framework.

🧱

Hard defenses

Seawalls, breakwaters, geotextile dikes, and sand nourishment protect cities and farmland. The 1830 Muhammad Ali Wall in Abu-Qir is an old example.

Zahra (2026)
🌿

Nature-based solutions

Restoring coastal wetlands and protecting sand dunes help the coast defend itself for the long term.

Nazarnia et al. (2020); Xu et al. (2021)
🗺️

Smart planning

Strict land-use rules can move new buildings away from high-risk flood areas. Policies should follow both land sinking and city growth.

Abdrabo & Hassaan (2015); Xu et al. (2021)
